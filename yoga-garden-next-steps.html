What I’d suggest

Here’s what I think would actually be worth building.

I don’t think you need a giant studio-management system with a hundred things you’ll never use. I’d start with the pieces that would actually take work off your plate, make things easier for clients, and give instructors what they need when they’re teaching.

We can always add more later if the studio grows into it. I just don’t think there’s any reason to build a pile of extras now simply because we can.

The Studio System

The working version of what you’ve just been clicking through.

The idea is to bring the booking, client information, waivers, schedule, instructors, waiting lists, payments, and the little bits of follow-up around all of that into one place.

Class schedule + bookingClients can see upcoming classes, check availability, and book themselves.
My GardenA simple client account with upcoming bookings, cancellations, payment status, and saved information.
Digital waiversBuilt into the first booking and stored with the client rather than living in a completely separate place.
Owner dashboardClasses, clients, waivers, instructors, payments, waiting lists, notifications, and basic studio settings.
Instructor viewTheir classes, rosters, check-in, waiver status, useful client information, and class-level payment status.
Cash + e-transfer trackingInstructors can record cash they receive if you allow it. E-transfers can be confirmed from your side.
Schedule changesChange an instructor or time, cancel one class, add something new, or make changes going forward.
Cancellation listsWhen a spot opens up, the system can work through the waiting list rather than you having to message everyone yourself.
Client emailsBooking confirmations, reminders, cancellation notices, open-spot messages, etc.
Setup + launchYour classes, instructors, settings, testing, and connection into the Wix site you already have.

And what I wouldn’t bother with right now: online card processing, memberships, packages, payroll, commissions, a point-of-sale system, or all the other bells and whistles you haven’t actually asked for. If one of those things becomes useful later, we can deal with it then.
