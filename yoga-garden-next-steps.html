What I think is worth building.

I don’t think The Yoga Garden needs a giant studio-management system with a hundred things you’ll never use. I’d start with the pieces that would actually make the day-to-day easier: booking, client information, waivers, the schedule, instructors, cancellation lists, payments, and the little bits of follow-up around all of that.

Clients would be able to book themselves, complete a waiver when needed, choose how they’re paying, and keep track of their upcoming classes through My Garden. You’d have one place to manage classes, clients, instructors, payments, waivers, and waiting lists. Instructors would have a much simpler view with the information they actually need to run their classes.
