What I’d suggest

Build the part that actually makes the studio easier to run.

I wouldn’t try to turn The Yoga Garden into a giant studio-management system. I’d build the pieces that take some of the repetitive work off your plate, make things easier for your clients, and give your instructors the information they actually need when they’re teaching.

The Studio System

What I’d build.

This would be the real, working version of the system you’ve just been clicking through.

Class schedule + booking Clients can see what’s coming up, check availability, and book themselves.
My Garden A simple client account with upcoming bookings, cancellations, and saved information.
Digital waivers Built into the first booking and stored with the client record.
Owner dashboard Classes, clients, instructors, waivers, waiting lists, notifications, and studio settings.
Instructor access Their classes, rosters, check-in, relevant client information, waiver status, and payment status.
Cash + e-transfer tracking Instructors can record cash they receive. E-transfers can be confirmed on the owner side.
Schedule control Change an instructor or time, cancel one class, add something new, or change future classes.
Cancellation lists When a spot opens, the system can work through the waiting list instead of you messaging everyone.
Client emails Booking confirmations, reminders, cancellations, and open-spot notifications.
Setup + launch Your classes, instructors, settings, testing, and connection into your existing Wix site.

What I’d leave out for now: online card processing, memberships, class packages, payroll, commissions, point-of-sale, and the other larger pieces you haven’t actually said you need. We can always add something later if it becomes useful.
