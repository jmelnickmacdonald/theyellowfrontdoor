Hi Genevieve

I ended up taking this a little further than I normally would before giving you a quote.

So much of my work is visual, and once I started thinking through what this could look like for you, I wanted to make sure I understood the whole sequence properly before I put a number on it.

I wanted to think through what happens when someone finds a class, books, fills out a waiver, comes back another week, cancels, or ends up on a waiting list. I also wanted to make sure I understood what you would actually need on your side, and what would genuinely be useful for an instructor.

It was much easier for me to map the whole thing visually than to try to work it all out in a list.

What you’re about to see is just that: a flat prototype. There’s no real backend, no database, no live client information, and no actual booking system running behind it yet. I built enough visually to work through the sequence and show you what I mean.

Where this went

That gave me somewhere to see the whole thing in context. The more I worked through it, though, the more I realized the part that could actually make your life easier was everything happening behind the website. That’s really what I wanted to show you.
