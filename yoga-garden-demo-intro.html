Hi Genevieve

I ended up taking this a little further than I normally would before giving you a quote.

So much of my work is visual that I wanted to work through the whole thing properly first: what happens when someone books, where the waiver fits, what happens if they cancel, what you need on your side, and what would actually be useful for an instructor.

It was easier for me to map the sequence out visually than try to price a fairly vague idea and figure out all of those pieces later.

Just so it’s clear: what you’re looking at is a flat prototype. There isn’t a real booking system, database, or client information behind it yet. I built enough of the front end to work through how I think it should all fit together.
