Hi Genevieve

I ended up thinking this through a little further than I normally would.

Once I started thinking about The Yoga Garden specifically, I wanted to work through the whole sequence properly — what a client sees, where the waiver fits, what happens when a class fills or someone cancels, what you need to control, and what an instructor actually needs access to.

So I built it out visually. Mostly because it was much easier for me to work through all of those pieces this way than to come to you with a long email full of “and then maybe this could happen…”

I wanted to make sure the idea actually made sense from beginning to end before I brought it to you.
i
One important thing before you start clicking: this is a flat visual prototype.

There’s no live booking system, database, real client information, or working backend behind this yet. The interactions below are here to show the sequence and how I imagine the finished system working. Everything is fake demo data.
