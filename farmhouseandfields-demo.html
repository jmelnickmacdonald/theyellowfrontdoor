A different kind of daycare

More country house than commercial facility.

Daycare here is intentionally small. The goal is not to fit as many dogs as possible into a room. It is to create a familiar place where dogs can play, settle, explore, and be known.

Farmhouse & Fields operates as a closed pack, with a small number of regular dogs and thoughtful introductions when a new space becomes available. There is no boarding, no grooming, and no constant shuffle of unfamiliar drop-ins.

The result is simple: more room, more consistency, and a day that feels a lot less like a facility.
