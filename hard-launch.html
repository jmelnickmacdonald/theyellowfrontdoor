Why the hard launch?

We opened in the middle of summer.

So now we're properly launching.

The Yellow Front Door has been quietly getting its feet under it for the last month or so, but we also opened in the middle of summer, when half the Valley seemed to be at the cottage, at the beach, in the garden, visiting family across the country, or saying “we should really get together before school starts” to approximately 14 people.

Now life is starting to settle back into something resembling a routine, and we're ready to really get moving.

We could spend our launch budget telling more people what we can do. We'd rather put pretty much all of it into doing the work and showing them. That gives us the chance to work with more businesses, build more useful things, and give a handful of projects a little more room than they might otherwise have had.

A few practical details

The straightforward version.

  • Hard Launch project spots are limited and apply to new project work.
  • We'll match your project investment dollar for dollar in additional Yellow Front Door work, up to a maximum match of $2,000.
  • The matched portion is additional scoped work, not cash back, a rebate, or a credit to use later.
  • We agree on the complete paid and matched scope before the project begins. We're not going to invent extra work simply to use up the match.
  • A project spot is secured once the scope is approved and the required project deposit is received.
  • Project fit, timing, and availability still matter. If we're not the right fit for the work, we'll say so.
