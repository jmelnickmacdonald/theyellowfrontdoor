Why the hard launch?

We opened in the middle of summer.

Excellent planning, really.

The Yellow Front Door has been quietly getting its feet under it for the last month or so, but we also opened when half the Valley seemed to be at the cottage, at the beach, in the garden, visiting family across the country, or saying “we should really get together before school starts” to approximately 14 people.

Now life is starting to settle back into something resembling a routine, and we're ready to properly launch.

We could spend our launch budget trying to tell more people what we can do. We'd rather put pretty much all of it into doing the work and showing them. That means working with more businesses, building more useful things, and giving a handful of projects a little more room than they might otherwise have had.

The small print

Nothing weird hiding down here.

  • Hard Launch project spots are limited and apply to new project work.
  • We'll match your project investment dollar for dollar in additional Yellow Front Door work, up to a maximum match of $2,000.
  • The matched portion is additional scoped work, not cash back, a rebate, or a credit to use later.
  • We agree on the complete paid and matched scope before work begins. We're not going to invent extra work simply to use up the match.
  • A spot is secured once the project scope is approved and the required project deposit is received.
  • Project fit, timing, and availability apply. If we're not the right fit for something, we'll tell you.
