Why the hard launch?

We opened in the middle of summer.

Excellent planning, really.

The Yellow Front Door has been quietly getting its feet under it for the last month or so, but we also opened when everyone seemed to be at the cottage, at the beach, in the garden, visiting family across the country, or saying “we should really get together before school starts” to approximately 14 people.

Now life is starting to settle back into something resembling a routine, and we're ready to properly launch.

The Yellow Front Door is new. We aren't. We could spend our launch budget trying to tell more people what we can do, but we'd rather put pretty much all of it into doing the work and showing them.

It gives us the chance to work with more businesses, build more useful things, meet more business owners, and give a handful of projects a little more room than they might otherwise have had.

A few practical details

Nothing weird hiding down here.

  • Hard Launch project spots are limited and apply to new project work.
  • We'll match your project investment dollar for dollar in additional Yellow Front Door work, up to a maximum match of $2,000.
  • The matched portion is additional scoped Yellow Front Door work. It isn't cash back, a rebate, or a credit to spend later.
  • We agree on the complete paid and matched scope before the project begins. We're not going to invent extra work simply to use up the match.
  • A project spot is secured once the scope is approved and the required project deposit is received.
  • Project fit, timing, and availability still matter. If we're not the right fit for something, we'll tell you.
