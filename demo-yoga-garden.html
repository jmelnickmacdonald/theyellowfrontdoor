The Yoga Garden

A bright studio, a lot of plants, and room to move.

The Yoga Garden is in Greenwood, with yoga, Pilates, heated classes, and strength-based movement on the schedule. Mats and props are there if you need them, and the plants around the studio are very much real.

Genevieve propagates plenty of them herself. When some are ready for a new home, they head out into the community and make room for the next batch.

If you have a plant that needs a new home, The Yoga Garden would love to be its next one.
