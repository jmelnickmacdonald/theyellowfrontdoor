Farmhouse & Fields

Dog Daycare — Brand & Digital Direction

A first look at Farmhouse & Fields. The colours, typography, photography, tone of voice, and foundation lines we will build from to create a warm, functional, and distinct online home.

Prepared for Melissa • September 2026 • Brand Direction v4

01   Brand Core

The Farmhouse already has a very clear, welcoming feel to it. The website does not need to invent one—it simply needs to showcase what Melissa has built: a small, consistent group of dogs, a comfortable and familiar space, and genuine care.

At the Centre

Low, consistent numbers. Familiar dogs. Lots of room to play. And Melissa, who knows each dog well enough to notice the little details, friendships, routines, and quirks.

The Farmhouse Atmosphere

The house, yard, barn, and fields are integral to the experience. It serves as a true "home away from home" for these pups and is what makes the experience completely unique.

03   Typography

Warm headings paired with clear, readable body structure that complements the existing script logo.

Fraunces

Headings & Feature Lines

Warm, distinctive serif font used for page titles, section headings, and impactful callouts without feeling overly rustic.

DM Sans

Body Copy & Interface

Clean, friendly, and practical. Used for navigation, instructions, forms, pricing, captions, and general reading.

04   Photography & Voice

Real Storytelling Photography

  • No Stock Photos: Use real photos throughout all pages.
  • Capture Real Moments: Muddy paws, goofy expressions, quiet moments, seasonal changes, and best friends.
  • Light Editing: Crop for layout needs, but keep color natural and authentic.

Authentic Brand Voice

  • Down-to-Earth: Warm, direct, with real stories about specific dogs.
  • No Hype Words: Skip generic terms like "premium," "curated," or endless dog puns.
  • Clear Instructions: Keep pricing, hours, policies, and wait-list steps straightforward.

06   Website Architecture

Proposed site structure ensuring key information is organized logically for both prospective and existing clients.

Page Purpose & Focus
01. Home First look at the Farmhouse, key benefits, photo highlights, and direct calls to action.
02. The Farmhouse Melissa's story, the property (house, yard, barn, fields), and how the daycare came to be.
03. Daycare Routines, hours, pricing, group sizes, requirements, and day-in-the-life details.
04. Gallery Dedicated space for photography showing seasonal changes, play, and quiet moments.
05. FAQs Direct answers to everyday questions prospective families ask before reaching out.
06. Join the Wait-list Clear explanation of onboarding, availability, and a simple dog application form.
07. Current Clients Direct link to Time To Pet portal for existing clients (booking, updates, invoices).
08. Contact Simple direct contact method for general inquiries.
