The reality

The business changes. The outside doesn't always keep up.

Maybe the website was built three years ago and barely resembles the business now. Maybe Google still has the wrong hours. Maybe every customer reply gets typed from scratch. Maybe you know what makes the business good, but putting it into words is another story entirely.

None of that means the business itself isn't working. Usually, it means you've been busy running it.

We work on the pieces around the business so what people see from the outside finally matches what you've built on the inside.
