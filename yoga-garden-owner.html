Digital waiver

How it works

Current version v1
1 · First booking A new client completes the waiver as part of registration.
2 · Saved to account The signed waiver, version, date, and acknowledgements are stored with the client record.
3 · Future bookings If the current waiver is already on file, the client skips that step.
If the waiver changes later: publish a new waiver version. Clients with an older version can be prompted to sign the updated waiver the next time they book or log in.

Waiting for a spot

The handoff

What happens automatically

Client cancels The class changes from full to one open spot.
First offer The first person waiting receives a time-limited email.
Claim or expire They claim the spot or the offer expires.
Next person If needed, the offer automatically moves down the queue.
You can still step in. The owner view shows who is waiting, who currently has an offer, when it expires, and lets you manually move the queue if needed.

After someone books

Immediately Booking confirmation with class, instructor, payment choice, location, and My Garden link.
First visit Confirmation notes that the waiver has been saved to the client account.
24 hours before Optional class reminder with arrival information.

Cancellation sequence

When a booking is cancelled

Cancellation confirmed The original client receives immediate confirmation.
Spot offered If someone is waiting, the first person receives a claim link.
Offer window They have 4 hours to claim it.
Confirmation Once claimed, they receive the normal booking confirmation.
