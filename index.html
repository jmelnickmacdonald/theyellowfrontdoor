Where we work

Based in Nova Scotia. Working across Canada and the U.S.

The Yellow Front Door is based on our little hobby farm in the Annapolis Valley, but most of our work happens remotely with businesses across Canada and the U.S. Some clients are just down the road; others are across the country or across the border. Wherever you are, we start by understanding your business, your customers, and what already exists before deciding what actually needs changing.

How it works

Three steps, no surprises.

01

We look.

Send us your website and a handful of touchpoints: your Google listing, social profiles, print pieces, emails, or client materials. We look at what's working and where the gaps are.

02

We plan.

Together, we figure out what matters most and make a plan. You know what you're getting, what it costs, and what happens next before we touch anything.

03

We build.

We do the work: website, copy, brand, marketing pieces, client materials, or systems. If you want ongoing help after that, we can stick around too.
