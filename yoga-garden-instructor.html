A quick note before you explore

You can look at this from three different sides of the studio.

ClientSee the website, classes, booking flow, and My Garden.
OwnerSee the behind-the-scenes side: classes, clients, waivers, payments, cancellations, waiting lists, instructors, and the things you’d be able to manage yourself.
InstructorSee the simpler class-day view, with rosters, check-in, useful client information, waiver status, and payment status.

Use the switcher at the top to move between the three views. I’d start on the client side and click through it as though you were actually booking a class, then have a look at the owner and instructor sides.

Everything here is still a static prototype with pretend data. It’s just meant to show how the pieces could work together and what the experience could look like.
